# Video-Type Routes

Select one primary route. Combine only the rules needed from a secondary route.

## Talking Head

Primary signal: spoken meaning and delivery.

Analyze transcript timing, filler words, repeated takes, false starts, silence, facial expression, gaze, framing, gestures, and audio consistency. Build the story from complete thoughts. Use jump cuts where natural, but protect word boundaries and add brief handles around breaths and emphatic gestures.

Use reframing, punch-ins, captions, keywords, screenshots, diagrams, or B-roll to reset attention when they clarify the message. Do not add visual changes at a fixed interval without a narrative reason.

Typical scoring priority: semantic value, delivery quality, emotional force, continuity, visual clarity.

## Screen Tutorial or Software Walkthrough

This is the primary route for the supplied examples. The source is a landscape screen capture placed inside a portrait composition, sometimes with a circular presenter overlay and emphasized captions.

Primary signal: completion and legibility of user actions. Analyze cursor movement, clicks, typing, menus, page changes, loading states, OCR, narration, and the region of interest. Segment by instructional steps rather than by scene cuts or silence alone.

Editing rules:

- Preserve context before an action and a short readable hold after the result appears.
- Accelerate repetitive typing, loading, scrolling, or cursor travel when comprehension is preserved.
- Use deterministic crop, pan, and zoom to keep the active control readable. Do not shrink an entire desktop until UI text becomes illegible.
- Keep cursor continuity across cuts. Avoid cutting between mouse-down and visible result.
- Use callouts, highlights, or magnifiers only on the active control.
- Place captions outside the active UI region. A presenter bubble is optional and must not cover controls, source text, or captions.
- Crop away captured platform chrome when the actual source video is available. The social-app interface visible in a reference screenshot is not part of the target design.

Typical scoring priority: instructional necessity, UI legibility, action completion, narration alignment, continuity.

## Product Ad

Primary signal: persuasive proof. Classify the ad as direct-response UGC, polished product demonstration, comparison, testimonial, or brand-led spot. This subtype controls pacing and visual polish.

Map every claim to visible product evidence or supplied substantiation. Build around hook, problem, mechanism, proof, payoff, and CTA, omitting stages the brief does not need. Prioritize clean product visibility, use-case clarity, transformation, credible reactions, and brand consistency.

Generate assets only for documented gaps. Label generated demonstrations internally and never imply that simulated behavior is recorded product evidence.

Typical scoring priority: proof strength, product visibility, message clarity, emotional force, platform fit.

## Interview or Podcast Repurpose

Primary signal: self-contained insight or tension. Search for claims, disagreement, surprise, vulnerability, useful frameworks, concise stories, and quotable lines. Ensure the selected clip makes sense without missing antecedents.

Remove moderator setup only when the answer remains intelligible. Use speaker-aware captions and preserve reaction shots when they add meaning. For multi-camera sources, prefer cuts motivated by speaker turns, reactions, or emphasis.

Typical scoring priority: standalone value, hook strength, delivery, context completeness, speaker continuity.

## Montage, Highlight Reel, or Mixed-Source Narrative

Primary signal: visual progression and rhythm. Analyze actions, motion direction, subject identity, shot scale, location, emotion, audio peaks, and visual similarity. Tag clips by narrative role and energy.

Construct an arc rather than a collection of individually strong shots. Match or intentionally contrast motion, eyelines, shapes, colors, and audio. Avoid near-duplicate shots and unexplained continuity breaks. Music beats may refine cut points after semantic ordering; they should not determine the story by themselves.

Typical scoring priority: visual quality, novelty, motion and emotional force, continuity, narrative contribution.

## Hybrid Examples

- Talking head plus software demo: primary route follows the viewer promise. Use screen-tutorial action rules for demonstration ranges and talking-head rules for explanation ranges.
- UGC product ad: product-ad structure with talking-head delivery and cleanup rules.
- Tutorial compiled from many sources: screen-tutorial legibility plus montage continuity and source attribution.
